# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Expo SDK 54, React Native 0.81, Expo Router, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage), NativeWind/Expo UI

## Users

Retail shoppers looking for high-end statement pieces, interior designers curating for clients, and wholesale buyers outfitting commercial spaces.

## Product Purpose

A digital showroom and eCommerce platform for discovering and purchasing premium furniture. Success means demonstrating a flawless, production-ready full-stack eCommerce experience (as a portfolio piece) that feels luxurious and trustworthy to the end user.

## Positioning

A curated gallery of avant-garde and timeless architectural furniture. Unlike standard mass-market catalogs, AtelierCarven treats each piece as a work of art, emphasizing negative space, premium materials, and bold structural forms.

## Operating Context

Users browse the catalog on mobile devices or desktop browsers, often comparing pieces, checking exact dimensions for room planning, and adding favorites before committing to a high-ticket purchase.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fully functional authentication, cart, checkout, and favorites.
- Dedicated admin portal for managing the furniture catalog, uploading gallery images, and reviewing activity logs.
- Backed by Supabase with strict Row Level Security (RLS).
- Must remain highly responsive and buttery smooth across all platforms.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone:** Refined, confident, minimalist, and architectural.
- **Identity:** High-end gallery aesthetic. Let the furniture photography serve as the primary color and texture of the interface.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing functional implementation for product listings, PDP (with multi-image gallery), cart, and auth.
- No real-world constraints or legacy branding to preserve (greenfield portfolio project).

## Product Principles

1. **Content is the Interface:** The furniture photography and typography must lead; chrome and heavy UI elements should recede.
2. **Tactile & Fluid:** Interactions must feel native, smooth, and immediate regardless of the device.
3. **Uncompromising Quality:** Every detail (from RLS security to edge-case UI states) must reflect a production-grade standard.
